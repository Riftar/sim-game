# First playable prototype plan

## Scope

Build one playable week in a Manggarai-inspired block for the ojek and toko careers. Connect a kos, a warung, a toko, a station gate, and two delivery points. Introduce four named NPCs and two short stories. Use the GDD's rupiah budget and first Sunday rent payment to tie the week together.

## Movement

- **On foot.** Free eight-direction movement with keyboard or controller. Use one interaction button for people, doors, counters, and work objects.
- **On a scooter.** Direct steering and braking on local streets. Park near a handoff and walk the last few steps along narrow alleys or into shops.
- **At work.** Walk to learn the workplace layout and reach meaningful stations. Routine sales resolve from the toko counter. The stockroom is a separate stop when a restock decision is due.
- **Between districts.** At a station or district exit, choose a destination, preview the fare and game time, and confirm. A short transition advances the clock. Add the first district connection in the vertical slice.
- **Repeat travel.** Offer optional route guidance and test a quick return home between jobs.

## Build order

1. **Block out the neighborhood.** Make walking, doors, scooter riding, and parking usable. Create one delivery with a walkable alley route and an alternative scooter route. Move a pickup under cover when it rains.
2. **Complete both work loops.** Add offer, pickup, and handoff for ojek. Add checkout, galon exchange, and restock for toko. Connect both to clock, wallet, and save settlement.
3. **Play the first week.** Start as a recent SMA graduate, finish the first paid task, meet a potential regular, and pay rent. Add two authored stories and reusable variants for weather, pickup timing, or available stock.
4. **Check the content workflow.** Add a fifth neighbor with existing scheduling and job rules. Tag an existing job as a campaign contribution and verify its settlement and retry behavior in the vertical slice.
5. **Test media side jobs after the core loop.** Try a paid market-stall video and a supervised field report on existing streets. Check that choosing footage and verifying a report send players through the neighborhood in different ways.

## Prototype checks

- Aim for 75% of test players to finish the first paid task within ten minutes without intervention.
- Verify that a normal workweek makes rent in both careers. Ask players to explain the difference between gross pay and take-home cash.
- Watch whether players find the alley, understand where to park, and reconsider a route when rain moves the pickup.
- Ask which neighbor players remember and what they chose differently on a later shift. Verify that a new ordinary customer can use an existing template through content data.
- Reload after a delivery, refund, and rent payment to verify each settles once.

See the [GDD](game-design.md) for career ladders, economy values, and later scope.
